# ChatGPT / OpenAI: desktop-pet brand discovery

## Identity and use context

**ChatGPT** is OpenAI's conversational AI product. In this project it is a familiar companion for someone using Codex: the pet should read as a calm, capable coding-and-thinking partner that can acknowledge completed work and balance changes without impersonating an official OpenAI character.

OpenAI describes itself as an AI research and deployment company. Its product material emphasizes conversational refinement, comparison, research, and making information easier to act on. This supports a desktop-pet personality that feels attentive, practical, quietly curious, and helpful rather than hyperactive or ceremonial.

## Visual cues grounded in sources

OpenAI's official design guidance describes a balance between technological precision and humanistic warmth, with rounded, approachable letterform qualities. ChatGPT's documented appearance system supports both light and dark themes and allows a neutral accent as well as color accents. For a tiny pet, translate that into a restrained charcoal-and-ivory base, one soft mint or teal accent, generous clean negative space, rounded forms, and crisp but not aggressive linework.

Use a compact chibi silhouette with an oversized expressive head, simple cream technical jacket, deep charcoal hair or hood, and a single mint/teal hair clip, collar light, or sleeve trim. The color accent should remain a small cue, not a direct product-color claim. Soft cel shading, large readable eyes, and a subtle thoughtful idle pose will preserve the friendly anime-Q feel of the existing pet while clearly being a new original character.

## Tone and interaction

The pet should feel observant, patient, clear, and ready to help with a task: a small nod or focused lean while refreshing, a warm relieved expression after work completes, and a reserved concern when the balance drops. Keep the energy calm enough for an always-on desktop presence. This personality guidance is an **inference** from the official descriptions of approachable human-centered design and conversational product use, not an official mascot specification.

## Domain motifs

Good non-verbal motifs are careful listening, drafting, reviewing, and organizing: a tiny rounded ear-piece, a compact notebook-shaped sleeve panel without text, a subtle collar light, or hands held as if considering a task. At pet size, the eye shape, rounded head silhouette, charcoal/ivory contrast, and one mint accent must carry the identity. Do not depend on labels, code, UI fragments, or a brand mark.

## Avoidances

Do not use the OpenAI Blossom, OpenAI or ChatGPT wordmarks, the letters GPT, readable slogans, copied UI panels, or a mascot that resembles an official endorsement. Do not build a pet from interlocking six-loop geometry or put that geometry on clothing, hair accessories, or props. Avoid a generic robot head, a literal chatbot bubble, a copied existing anime character, heavy neon cyberpunk, or blue-haired maid styling that could be confused with the retained DeepSeek option.

## Evidence and confidence

- **High confidence:** OpenAI's brand guidance explicitly frames the visual ethos as precision plus human warmth and says OpenAI/ChatGPT names, marks, logos, and design elements are protected; the guidance prohibits altered/unauthorized logo use and confusing affiliation.
- **High confidence:** ChatGPT offers light/dark appearance and a range of configurable accent colors, including a neutral choice. Therefore this pet should use an original, adaptable palette rather than claim one mandatory ChatGPT color.
- **Medium confidence / inference:** A calm chibi assistant is a suitable translation of ChatGPT's conversation, research, and refinement-oriented use. OpenAI does not publish an official ChatGPT mascot in these sources.
